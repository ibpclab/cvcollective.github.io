Founding Members:

* ** [Neurovascular Physiology Lab (https://www.nvplaboratory.net/)** - *Indiana University*
  Focus: The NVPL takes a broad approach to preventing and treating cardiovascular disease, studying blood flow, vessel function, and the neural pathways that control blood pressure in both healthy adults and patient populations. A central focus is understanding how everyday health behaviors — exercise, diet, and sleep — shape cardiovascular health, with an eye toward translating that work into real-world prevention strategies.
